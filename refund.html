Nestify Refund Policy

Last updated: 18 November 2026 · Elton Sibara (Nova Software Dev), South Africa

This policy covers Nestify plans bought on novasoftwaredev.co.za. A plan is a licence that unlocks Nestify on one computer for one month or one year. Nestify's 14-day free trial does not cost anything and needs no payment.

Changed your mind

You can ask for a full refund within 7 days of buying a plan, as long as the unlock code has not been used to unlock Nestify. Email us with your order reference and Computer ID, and we will refund you.

The code does not work

If a code you paid for does not unlock Nestify on the computer it was made for, tell us. We will try to fix it first, for example by making a new code for the same computer. If we cannot fix it within 7 days of your purchase, you can ask for a full refund.

Renewals

Monthly and yearly plans are paid again by you each time they run out. You can stop renewing at any time. We do not refund the unused part of a month or year, except as set out above.

Wrong computer or double payment

If you paid twice, or bought a plan for the wrong Computer ID, email us. We will correct it or refund the extra payment.

How refunds are paid

Refunds are paid back to the card or bank account you paid with, through Paystack. They can take several working days to show in your account, depending on your bank.

Your rights

Nothing in this policy takes away any rights you have under South Africa's Consumer Protection Act.

How to ask for a refund

Email eltonsibara1@gmail.com with your order reference (it starts with NST, and you get it on the order page) and the Computer ID from Nestify (Help > Your plan).
