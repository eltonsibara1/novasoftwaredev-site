Nestify Privacy Policy

Last updated: 11 November 2026 · Elton Sibara (Nova Software Dev), South Africa

Nestify is a Windows app that turns house plans into 3D houses. This page explains what information Nestify and this website use, and why. In short: your houses and drawings stay on your own computer. Nestify does not show ads, does not track you, and does not sell anything about you.

Your projects stay on your computer

The houses, plans, pictures and files you make in Nestify are saved only where you choose to save them. They are never uploaded to us. If you use a team project, it is saved in a shared folder that you and your team pick (for example OneDrive, Dropbox or a network drive), not on our servers.

What Nestify sends over the internet

  • Your Computer ID, a short code such as K7QXM-2RDTA, made from your computer's Windows ID so it cannot be traced back to you. Nestify sends it to our shop to check whether a plan was bought for this computer and to fetch your licence code. It contains no name, email or files.
  • Update check (website copy only): Nestify reads a small version file from this website to see if a newer version exists. Copies from the Microsoft Store are updated by the Store instead.
  • Online library: when you browse free 3D models and materials, Nestify downloads them from Poly Haven (polyhaven.com). Poly Haven receives a normal web request, like visiting a website.
  • Exchange rates: when you show prices in another currency, Nestify reads the rates from open.er-api.com.

Nestify does not collect analytics or crash reports, and it does not read files you have not opened in it.

When you buy a plan

Payments are handled by our payment provider. We never see or store your card or bank login details. For each order we keep the Computer ID, the plan, the amount, the date and the payment reference, so that your licence code can be made and given again if you need it. If you email us, we use your email address only to answer you.

This website

To count visitors, the website keeps a daily anonymous number made from your connection (it changes every day and cannot identify you) and the country your visit came from. Ratings and reviews you post are shown publicly with the name you enter. Downloads of Nestify are counted the same way.

Sharing

We do not sell, rent or share your information, except with the payment provider that processes your payment, or when the law requires it.

Children

Nestify is made for adults planning or designing houses. We do not knowingly collect information from children.

Your rights

Under South Africa's Protection of Personal Information Act (POPIA) you can ask what we keep about you, and ask us to correct or delete it. Email eltonsibara1@gmail.com with your Computer ID (in Nestify: Help > Your plan).

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.
